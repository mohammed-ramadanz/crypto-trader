My practice trades

Is the advice any good?

Each Buy or Sell call is checked against what the price really did afterwards. A Buy is right if the price went up, a Sell is right if it went down. Hold calls aren't scored.

A coin toss gets about 50%. If these scores sit near 50%, the advice is no better than guessing. Treat it as a learning aid, not a crystal ball.

What the labels mean

BUY: the price looks low compared with its recent average and has started to recover.
HOLD: no strong signal. Doing nothing is a valid choice.
SELL: the price has risen fast or sits well above its recent average. A drop often follows.
Fee: each practice trade costs 0.1%, the same as a standard Binance spot trade.
